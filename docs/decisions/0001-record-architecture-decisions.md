# 1. Record architecture decisions

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-19

## Context

The project's decisions and their reasons lived in a design narrative and a
development log, mixed with findings and progress notes. A reader who wants to know
why something is built the way it is had to read the whole story.

## Decision

Record each significant decision as an Architecture Decision Record in this
directory: numbered, never renumbered, with its context, the options considered,
the decision and its consequences. A replaced decision is kept and marked
superseded, with a link to its replacement. Decisions taken before this record
existed carry the date they were actually taken.

## Consequences

Findings — things observed in use — are not recorded here. What they changed shows
up in the decisions they led to and in [`evaluation.md`](../evaluation.md).
